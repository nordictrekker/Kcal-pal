# MyFitnessPal & Lose It!: What Users Love, Hate, and Request

**Research note on sources:** WebSearch returned rich synthesized summaries (with quotes) across all target sources. However, WebFetch was blocked with **HTTP 403 Forbidden on essentially every consumer/forum domain attempted** — including the MyFitnessPal community forum, Trustpilot, ResetEra, Slashdot, Cronometer forums, Nutrola, MyNetDiary, ChoosingTherapy, and HootFitness. The findings below are therefore drawn from search-result synthesis and snippet-level quotes rather than full-page fetches. Quotes attributed to specific URLs are paraphrased/snippet-level unless marked verbatim. This is the main reliability caveat.

## 1. WHAT USERS LOVE

### MyFitnessPal
- **Huge food database (~20M+ foods)** — the single most-cited reason people stay despite frustrations. It has nearly every packaged and restaurant food, so logging "just works." (https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/lose-it-vs-myfitnesspal, https://www.mynetdiary.com/myfitnesspal-alternatives.html)
- **Barcode scanner** (when it was free) — repeatedly called "a lifesaver for items that came packaged." Long-time users said they specifically recommended MFP to friends *because* of the free scanner. (https://www.techradar.com/health-fitness/i-tracked-everything-i-ate-for-a-week-with-myfitnesspal-heres-what-happened)
- **Recipe Importer** — pulls recipes from recipe websites and matches ingredients to the database; users say this is something they "miss when switching to other apps." Bulk ingredient import is heavily used. (https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032623231, https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/729/bulk-ingredient-import-for-recipes)
- **Saved Meals / Quick Add / copy-meal** — power users rely on saved meal sets and quick-add calories to speed repeat logging. (https://blog.myfitnesspal.com/copy-and-remember-meals/, https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032272432)
- **Device sync breadth** — syncs with most fitness wearables/trackers. (https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/lose-it-vs-myfitnesspal)

### Lose It!
- **Genuinely functional free tier** — the standout love. Users can "track calories and macros, scan barcodes, sync Fitbit/Garmin, log exercise, and monitor weight — all without entering a credit card." Premium adds convenience, it doesn't gate core function. (https://nutriscan.app/blog/posts/lose-it-pricing-2026-free-vs-premium-2b4e921555)
- **Clean, beginner-friendly UI** — consistently described as simpler and less cluttered than MFP. (https://fitia.app/learn/article/best-weight-loss-app-2025/)
- **"Snap It" photo meal logging + AI logging** — Lose It's own data claims AI/photo/voice logging drove 6% more weight loss, 3.5x faster meal logging, and 2x more foods logged. Users like the speed. (https://www.barchart.com/story/news/31984516, https://www.amyfoodjournal.com/blog/lose-it-app-review)
- **Streaks, challenges, social feed, calorie "budget" framing** — gamification and the budget metaphor are praised as motivating. (https://www.amyfoodjournal.com/blog/lose-it-app-review)
- **One-click "previous meals"** — Lose It's previous-meals UX (whole meal repopulates with one click) is so liked that **MFP users explicitly request MFP copy it.** (https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/5014)

## 2. WHAT USERS HATE

### MyFitnessPal

**A. Barcode scanner moved behind paywall (the 2022 flashpoint — still the #1 grievance)**
- Effective **Oct 1, 2022**, the previously-free barcode scanner became Premium-only. Backlash was "immediate and intense." (https://news.slashdot.org/story/22/08/25/1955238/)
- The Verge's Antonio G. Di Benedetto called it an **"egregious disservice"** to users. (search synthesis; Verge via https://news.slashdot.org/story/22/08/25/1955238/)
- On social media users called the company's pattern **"a constant string of 'fuck you' to consumers."** (https://news.slashdot.org/story/22/08/25/1955238/)
- An op-ed title captures the mood: **"Hey MyFitnessPal: We're Not Paying for a Damn Barcode Scanner."** (https://punishedbacklog.com/hey-myfitnesspal-were-not-paying-for-a-damn-barcode-scanner/)
- r/loseit users discussed mass-switching to **Cronometer, Lose It, and Macros** over it. Framed as the moment MFP "felt greedy"; many "felt betrayed" because they'd recommended it for the free scanner. (https://www.resetera.com/threads/...625390/, search synthesis)
- Context that fuels resentment: Under Armour bought MFP for $475M (2015), sold to **Francisco Partners** for $345M (2020); to recoup, FP paywalled barcode scanner, custom macro goals, and detailed nutrient tracking. (https://news.slashdot.org/story/22/08/25/1955238/)

**B. Ad load on the free tier (most frequent app-store complaint after paywall)**
- Free tier estimated at **~6–12 ad impressions per logging session**; reviewers call the app "outdated," "ad-choked," and "paywalled to death." (https://www.hootfitness.com/blog/why-users-are-switching-from-myfitnesspal-and-what-they-re-choosing-instead, https://nutrola.app/en/blog/why-does-myfitnesspal-have-so-many-ads)
- **Full-screen interstitial ads fire mid-action** — between logging food, switching tabs, or opening the diary; plus auto-play video ads in the feed. (https://nutrola.app/en/blog/why-does-myfitnesspal-have-so-many-ads)
- Forum thread titles tell the story: **"Stop the full screen ads at lunch,"** "Gotta go! Ads in the diary page," "Advertising tactics are causing a negative impact on your app." Users report the only fix is to "close the app and hope you can complete logging before it pops up again." (https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/discussion/10870884/, https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/discussion/10384159/, https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/discussion/10871838/)
- Ad complaints are "among the most frequent" 1–2★ reasons on both App Store and Play Store; words like "unusable," "constant," "worse than ever." (search synthesis of app store reviews)
- Note: some users now report **ads even appearing in the paid version.** (https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/discussion/10906338/ad-in-premium-version)

**C. Database accuracy / duplicate entries (chronic, structural complaint)**
- Crowdsourced DB = duplicates, wrong macros, outdated reformulated-product data. Cited example: **Chick-fil-A waffle fries have ~25 entries, most wrong, half bearing a (supposedly "verified") green check.** (https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/discussion/10873710/clean-up-remove-duplicates-food-listings, https://www.mynetdiary.com/best-calorie-tracker-database-accuracy.html)
- Validation study (J. Medical Internet Research, 2020): MFP **underestimated protein ~7.8% and carbs ~6.4%** vs lab reference; weak correlations for cholesterol (ρ=0.51) and sodium (ρ=0.53). (https://doaj.org/article/5f4740e901364065a8ce0d18bbe6f514)
- Blunt user verdict surfaced in search: the database is **"corrupted and almost useless."** (https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/comment/18204)

**D. Price hikes + billing/cancellation pain**
- Premium now **$19.99/mo or $79.99–$99.99/yr**; a mid-2024 price increase plus heavier late-2024 ad frequency correlate with MFP **dropping ~½ star** in tracked review sentiment. (https://www.fitbudd.com/post/myfitnesspal-app-cost, https://nutrola.app/en/blog/we-analyzed-50000-calorie-tracker-reviews-what-users-actually-complain-about-2026)
- **1.5★ on PissedConsumer (129 reviews)** vs 4.7★ App Store / 4.4★ Play — the gap is driven by billing, refund, and cancellation grievances. Complaints: can't find a way to cancel, paid premium that "stopped allowing them to use the app," denied refunds. (https://myfitnesspal.pissedconsumer.com/review.html, https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/san-francisco/profile/online-shopping/myfitnesspal-1116-539525/complaints)

**E. UX/quality regression**
- Users call it "horrible now," "a shell of itself." Complaints: can't disable the home-feed/news feed; "recent foods" doesn't prioritize most-used items. (https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/comment/18204, https://alternativeto.net/software/myfitnesspal/about)

### Lose It!
- **Macro tracking moved behind paywall** — recurring Reddit complaint that macros used to be free. (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/loseit.com, search synthesis)
- **Ads "sometimes disguised as 'gifts.'"** (search synthesis / Trustpilot)
- **Database also has duplicates / miscalculated items** — "not managed." (search synthesis)
- **Sync limitations** — no Samsung Watch sync; multi-device limits noted. (search synthesis)
- **Recent UI redesign criticized** as more cluttered. (search synthesis)
- **Auto-renew / refund / cancellation friction** — users report being charged with no advance notice; Lose It deflects refunds to Apple ("transaction processed by Apple, not Lose It"); BBB and Apple-discussions threads show this is a recurring pattern. Cancellation isn't intuitive (must cancel in device settings, not in-app). (https://www.bbb.org/us/ma/boston/profile/mobile-apps/lose-it-0021-188207/complaints, https://discussions.apple.com/thread/8025432, https://nutriscan.app/blog/posts/lose-it-7-day-free-trial-cancel-iphone-android-7c9dc5644a)

## 3. FEATURE REQUESTS (repeated asks)

- **One-click "copy previous day / previous meal"** — MFP users explicitly want Lose It–style previous-meals: whole meal repopulates with one click, no multi-add/copy-paste dance. Most-requested logging-speed ask for MFP. (https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/5014, https://blog.myfitnesspal.com/copy-and-remember-meals/)
- **Copy an entire *day*** — currently unsupported in MFP (copying works at meal level only). (https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032622131)
- **Verified / curated database entries** — users want professionally-reviewed entries (the Fitia/Cronometer model) to escape crowdsourced duplicate chaos. (https://www.mynetdiary.com/best-calorie-tracker-database-accuracy.html, https://fitia.app/learn/article/top-12-nutrition-tracking-apps-2026/)
- **Scan label → auto-create custom food** (vs barcode lookup only). (https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/5014)
- **Offline logging** with locally-cached barcode/food DB — frequently requested; most free apps require connectivity. (https://nutrola.app/en/blog/what-is-the-best-calorie-tracker-that-works-offline)
- **Ability to disable the home/news feed.** (https://alternativeto.net/software/myfitnesspal/about)
- **Better "recent/frequent foods" ranking.** (https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/comment/18204)
- **Micronutrient / vitamin & mineral tracking** in free tier (people leave for Cronometer to get it). (https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/comment/18204)
- Commonly-valued table-stakes features users look for: **water tracking, dark mode/themes.** (https://www.mobileappdaily.com/products/best-free-calorie-tracking-apps)

## 4. LOGGING-FRICTION QUIT POINTS

These are the moments that make people abandon tracking:
1. **Ads interrupting the log itself** — full-screen ad at "log lunch" is described as literally preventing task completion; reviewers say ads "lead to giving up on completing food logs or weigh-ins." (https://www.choosingtherapy.com/myfitnesspal-review/, https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/discussion/10870884/)
2. **Weighing/measuring food** — "felt laborious," "a bit of a faff"; weighing rice/pasta/homemade portions is a top reason people quit. One user "couldn't deal with the chore of logging all their food." (https://healthunlocked.com/weight-loss-support/posts/135831123/, https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/anyone-use-myfitnesspal.2399190/)
3. **Database friction** — picking the right entry among many wrong duplicates adds cognitive load every meal. (https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/discussion/10873710/)
4. **Losing the free barcode scanner** — removed the one shortcut that made packaged-food logging fast; cited as why people quit MFP entirely. (https://news.slashdot.org/story/22/08/25/1955238/)
5. **Repeat-meal friction** — without slick copy/previous-meal, re-logging the same daily meals feels redundant. (https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/5014)

## 5. PRICING / MONETIZATION SENTIMENT

- **The hard line: people will NOT pay for a barcode scanner.** That specific paywall is the most-resented monetization move in the category and triggered defections. (https://punishedbacklog.com/..., https://news.slashdot.org/story/22/08/25/1955238/)
- **Paywalling *previously-free* core features reads as "greedy"/"betrayal"** — the sequencing (free → paywalled) matters more than the absolute price. (search synthesis; https://www.hootfitness.com/blog/...)
- **What people WILL pay for:** genuinely additive premium (advanced macro/nutrient analysis, meal planner, AI meal-photo scan, ad removal) — *if* free still covers core logging. Lose It is the praised model: "Premium adds convenience features rather than gating core functionality." (https://nutriscan.app/blog/posts/lose-it-premium-worth-it-2026-who-should-pay-4eb070ea9d, https://nutriscan.app/blog/posts/lose-it-pricing-2026-free-vs-premium-2b4e921555)
- **Price tolerance:** MFP Premium at $19.99/mo is widely seen as overpriced; Lose It Premium at **$39.99/yr (~$3.33/mo)** is "the clearest reason to recommend it for budget-conscious users." Lose It lifetime ~$299.99 (often discounted to $149.99 on Black Friday) is also discussed favorably. (https://www.fitbudd.com/post/myfitnesspal-app-cost, https://nutriscan.app/blog/posts/lose-it-pricing-2026-free-vs-premium-2b4e921555)
- **Ad-removal is a real willingness-to-pay driver** specifically because the free-tier ad load is so heavy. (https://nutrola.app/en/blog/why-does-myfitnesspal-have-so-many-ads)
- **Billing transparency is a trust tax for both apps** — auto-renew without notice + hard/opaque cancellation generates 1★ reviews and BBB/PissedConsumer complaints for MFP and Lose It alike. (https://myfitnesspal.pissedconsumer.com/review.html, https://www.bbb.org/us/ma/boston/profile/mobile-apps/lose-it-0021-188207/complaints)

## Quick synthesis for product use
The clearest market opening both apps leave open: **a fast, low-friction logging flow (one-tap copy previous day, label-scan to custom food, offline barcode) + a clean/verified database + honest monetization (core logging free, ads light, additive premium, transparent billing).** MFP's reputation damage is concentrated in (1) the barcode paywall, (2) ad interruptions during logging, and (3) database duplicate noise. Lose It is the well-regarded counter-model on free-tier generosity and simplicity, but shares the database-duplicate and auto-renew/refund grievances.

**Reliability caveat repeated:** quotes are snippet/search-synthesis level because WebFetch was 403-blocked on all forum/review domains; the paywall facts (dates, $ figures, Verge quote, ownership history) are corroborated across multiple independent results and are high-confidence, while exact verbatim wording of individual app-store/Reddit reviews should be re-verified by directly opening the cited URLs before publication.
